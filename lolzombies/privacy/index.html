Privacy · lolZombies

Privacy policy

Publisher: Monoraton Games
Effective date: October 11, 2026
Applies to: lolZombies for Android, including version 5.1.2.

lolZombies does not automatically send gameplay diagnostics to Monoraton Games. It keeps a limited diagnostic journal on your device. Where the beta report feature is available, a player can choose to prepare and send a report through their own email app.

Information used on your device

The game processes gameplay state such as level, score, health, and ammunition to run the game. Its diagnostic journal records recent gameplay events, their times, performance samples such as frame rate and frame duration, and whether the previous session ended cleanly. The journal is stored in the app’s private data area as beta_session.json; it is not a cloud leaderboard or an account profile.

The journal keeps bounded recent history (up to 120 events and 90 performance samples), and is rewritten during play. Recent information from an interrupted session may be loaded into memory to help explain a problem. The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location, or Internet access, and does not use advertising or analytics services.

Optional bug reports and email

Version 5.1.2 keeps its beta bug-report control hidden during normal play. The following explains how its report code handles information if used in a build where reporting is available.

If you use the bug-report feature, the game prepares a report with your comments, app version, report and session times, recent diagnostic history, current game state, and device details such as manufacturer, model, device name, Android version, Android API level, and screen dimensions. These device details describe the device configuration; the report does not request an advertising ID, IMEI, or device serial number.

You choose whether to send the report using your email app. Nothing is sent automatically by the game. If you send it, the receiving support mailbox receives the report and the sender information supplied by your email service, including your email address. We use support messages to respond, investigate bugs, and improve the game.

Version 5.1.2 uses a legacy studio support mailbox for its built-in report composer. For privacy requests and general support, use monoratongames@gmail.com. If an email app cannot be opened, the game copies the report to your clipboard and tells you; you decide where to paste it. Clipboard handling depends on your operating system and other apps.

Please review a report before sending and avoid including passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information in your comments.

Third-party services and sharing

The game uses Python, Kivy, and supporting open-source libraries to run on the device. It has no integrated ad network, analytics service, account service, or automatic crash-reporting service. We do not sell support messages or use them for advertising. Email providers process messages you choose to send under their own terms and privacy policies. Information may also be disclosed where legally required.

Android backup: Version 5.1.2 allows Android’s system backup. Depending on your device, account, and backup settings, the operating system may back up or transfer local app files, including the diagnostic journal. This is separate from automatic reporting to Monoraton Games. Manage those copies through your device and backup provider.

This website: These pages do not add analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, or remote fonts. GitHub Pages hosts the website and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Visiting the policy in a browser is separate from playing the game.

Retention and deletion

Local diagnostics remain in the app’s data area until overwritten or removed. You can clear the game’s app data in Android settings to remove that local data. Backup copies and restored data may remain subject to your backup settings; clearing local data does not necessarily delete a provider’s backup. Gameplay checkpoints are held in memory rather than a separate persistent save service.

Support emails may remain in the support mailbox while needed to respond, investigate the issue, and keep relevant support records. To request deletion of a message you sent, contact monoratongames@gmail.com with enough information to locate it. We can address copies under our control; your own email and backup providers control their copies. The game has no player accounts to delete.

Children and audience

lolZombies contains zombie combat and comic violence. This policy does not assign an age rating; consult the game’s store listing for its rating and intended audience when available. The game does not ask for a player’s age or operate a child account system. If a child has sent personal information in a support message, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its deletion.

Security

Local diagnostics use the app’s private storage area and the protections provided by Android. Support messages are handled through email services. No storage or transmission method guarantees absolute security; avoid including sensitive information in reports.

Policy updates

We will publish changes at this permanent URL and update the effective date. If the game’s data practices change, its disclosures will need to reflect those changes.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact Monoraton Games at monoratongames@gmail.com.
